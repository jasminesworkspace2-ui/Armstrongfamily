Stephen R. Waldrop

Individual evidence file separating Stephen's documented transactions from broader Waldrop family associations.

Primary-source control

Rankin County · Deed Book 4 · p. 435

March 25, 1839. The archive preserves Patience → Rachel → Tom and Patience → Clarissa → William, Amanda, Harriet from the 1839 reconstruction. The recovered transcription names Stephen with the other seven children as joint grantees of Patience and her named descendants. Stephen's separately documented 1827 receipt of Patience, Rachel and Thomas and his August 1839 sale of Rachel and Bryant remain distinct transactions and must not be rewritten as a separate March 1839 allotment.

Documented direct recipient

1827 · Hinds County, Mississippi

Hezekiah Waldrop transferred Patience, Rachel and Thomas to Stephen. The recorded values were $300 for Patience, $500 for Rachel and $200 for Thomas.

DOCUMENTED FACT · ORIGINAL RECORD TRACK
1839 · Rankin County

The heir distribution

The later instrument recites the 1795 gift of Patience to Martha “Patsy” Davenport Waldrop and names Patience's increase, including Clarissa, Rachel, Tom, William, Amanda and Harriet. The instrument names Stephen as one of eight joint grantees; it does not state a separate Stephen-only allotment in the recovered transcription.

August 19, 1839 · Holmes County

Rachel and Bryant → Howell Prestridge

Stephen sold Rachel for $850 and her son Bryant for $600 to Howell Prestridge. This is a named, person-specific transaction after the March 25 joint conveyance. It documents Rachel and Bryant leaving Stephen's legal trail, but it does not by itself explain how Stephen obtained authority to sell their interests after the joint conveyance; that legal step remains a document target.

The elderly “P.” clue

Keep the relationships exact
