A Guide to the Archive

Explore the Archive.

There is more than one way into this family history. You can begin with yourself, read the story, follow the research, or go straight to the documents. This page simply helps you choose the door.

Where should I begin?

You do not need to understand the whole archive before you enter it.

Start with the question you actually have. Use the family tree for names and relationships, the estate network to follow historical connections, the Research Archive for detailed investigations, and the Paper Trail or gallery to read or view the records.

How to read this archive

This is a living research archive. Not every statement carries the same level of evidence. A primary record may document a fact; several independent clues may support a connection; a research lead may point toward a person or relationship without closing the documentary gap; and some questions remain open.
