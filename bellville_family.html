★ The Heart of This Archive Easter Bell Nichols July 4, 1838 — December 17, 1935 · Bellville, Texas · Age 97

Born enslaved. Died free. She married Joseph Nichols on December 18, 1865 — six months after Juneteenth. Together they had at least six children. She outlived slavery, Reconstruction, Jim Crow, and two world wars. Her original name before marriage was Easter Bell — the Bell surname came from the Bell family of Bellville, in whose household she had been enslaved.
