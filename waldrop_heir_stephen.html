July 5, 1839 · recorded August 19, 1839 · Holmes County

Rachel and Briant → Howell Prestridge

The reviewed sale record shows Stephen R. Waldrop → Howell Prestridge for $850, conveying Rachel with a boy whose name reads Briant in the research transcription. The record does not clearly state that Briant was Rachel's son. That relationship must therefore remain unresolved here. This transaction follows the March 25 joint conveyance and is a key downstream legal event.
