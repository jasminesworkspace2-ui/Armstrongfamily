Nancy Frances “Fanny” Waldrop Nichols

1802 · daughter · Mississippi → Texas

Identity control

Individual research file

Fanny is the documented Waldrop–Nichols family bridge. She married John Nichols in 1821 in Lawrence County, Mississippi. Their later Texas history belongs to the downstream migration story; the marriage itself should not be relocated to Texas. This relationship does not prove that Patience, Rachel, Clarissa, Joseph, Easter, Rendy, or any other enslaved person moved with them.

The distribution instrument is the starting point for this dossier. It recites the earlier 1795 gift of Patience to Martha “Patsy” Davenport Waldrop and names Patience's increase, including Clarissa, Rachel, Tom, William, Amanda and Harriet. This heir's exact allotment must be extracted from the instrument rather than inferred.
