Hjordis L. Corbin

Born January 20, 1960 · Living
The Arc

Hjordis L. Corbin was born on January 20 1960. She is the daughter of Margie Nell Ballard Corbin and Norman Caswell Corbin, the granddaughter of Eva Armstrong Ballard and James Wilson Ballard Sr., and the great-granddaughter of Hellen Nichols Armstrong and John Armstrong.

She carries the direct maternal line — unbroken, mother to daughter — from Molley in Union County South Carolina in 1793 through eight generations to the present. That line passes through Patience, Clarissa, Easter Bell Nichols, Hellen Nichols Armstrong, Eva Armstrong Ballard, and Margie Nell Ballard Corbin before reaching her.

Hjordis is the living link between nine generations of documented family history and the generation that comes after. Her daughter Jasmine M. Lee — born January 25 1982 — is the ninth generation and the compiler of this archive.

Hjordis's AncestryDNA kit — SQ8499767 — is one of the two active DNA kits used in this research. Her DNA has confirmed 46 ThruLines matches through Clarissa as the common ancestor, 46 matches through the Waldrop family line, and segments on chromosomes 8, 11, and 17 connecting to the Nichols-Reese community network. Her DNA is the biological thread that connects documentary research to living descendants.

She knew Eva. She heard the stories. She is the reason this research could be done at all.
