Two Ways to Use This Archive

For the Family.

This archive was built for two audiences. Family members who just want to know the story — and researchers who want to see every document. Both of you are welcome here. This page helps you find what you need.

Help Build This Archive

Do You Have a Photo, Story, or Memory?

This archive was built from documents — but documents do not capture everything. If you have a photograph of any family member, a letter, a memory passed down from a grandparent, or a story about any person in this archive — we want it. Send it to jlee@dockellisfoundation.com with the person's name and any dates you know. Every contribution will be credited and preserved.

If you think you may be related but are not sure — go to the Start Here page and use the relationship tool. Or go directly to Contact.
