The heir distribution

The later instrument recites the 1795 gift of Patience to Martha “Patsy” Davenport Waldrop and names Patience's increase, including Clarissa, Rachel, Tom, William, Amanda and Harriet. Stephen's exact share should be read from the instrument rather than inferred from the family relationship.

Rachel leaves Stephen's legal trail

Keep the relationships exact
