Who This Family Is

If your family carries the name Corbin, Ballard, Armstrong, Nichols, or Pink — or if a DNA test led you here — you may be looking at your own family history.

This archive traces one African American family from an enslaved woman named Patience in Newberry County, South Carolina in 1795 — all the way to the present day. Nine confirmed generations. Every name found in a primary source document — a deed, a death certificate, a census record, a marriage index, a slave schedule, a court filing.

The family's roots are in Bellville, Austin County, Texas — a town built on a land grant whose enslaved community became your family after emancipation in 1865. Before Texas the trail runs through Mississippi and all the way back to South Carolina.
