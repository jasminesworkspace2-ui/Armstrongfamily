Bessie Rawls · Funeral Program · August 31, 1973

Who She Was

Bessie Rawls was known as Aunt Bessie to all of Eva Armstrong Ballard's children for the entirety of their lives in Orange County, California. She was a warm and present figure in the family — the kind of person whose title of aunt was earned through love and consistency, whether or not blood has yet confirmed the exact relationship.

She is believed to have come from Texas, which aligns with the Armstrong and Ballard family's own Texas roots. She made her way to California as so many of this family did — following the same migration path from the South to Los Angeles and Orange County that defined a generation of Black families in the mid-20th century.

Research Status · Open

Bessie Rawls is documented here as a close family member whose exact bloodline connection to Eva Armstrong Ballard has not yet been confirmed by a primary source. She was born in Texas in 1890 — the same generation as Eva's parents. She is believed to be Eva's aunt or cousin. The California Death Index and Social Security records confirm her identity. The California County death certificate has not yet been obtained and may name her survivors or next of kin.
