What this archive is about

The names came first.
The story came after.

For generations, the people in this family were recorded in documents that were never created to tell their stories. Enslaved people appear in deeds, wills, inventories, schedules, tax records, and court files — often only as property.

This archive brings those records together and follows the people beyond the transaction: who they were, who they were connected to, where they lived, what happened to them, and what we still do not know.

The goal is not to make the story cleaner than the evidence allows. The goal is to preserve it accurately enough that the next generation can keep going.
