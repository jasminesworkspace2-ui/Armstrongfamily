Communities We Are Researching
Bellville · Austin County · Texas

The Nichols family — Easter Bell, Joseph, Henry, Allen, Oscar, Annie, Josephine, Hellen — lived in and around Bellville, Texas from approximately 1865 through the early 20th century. If your family was in Bellville during this period, we likely share history. The Jeff Cemetery in Bellville holds several Nichols family members. We are actively researching the John Nichols 1864 probate file and the Joseph Nichols household in the Austin County census.

Newberry County · South Carolina

Patience — our 5th great-grandmother — was given to Patty Waldrop in Newberry County on August 4 1795 in a deed executed by Francis Davenport Sr. Her mother Molley was held by James Sims Sr. in Union County in 1793. The Waldrop, Sims, Davenport, and Turner families of this community are all connected to this research. DNA matches through Claiborne Waldrop's branch have been confirmed.

Smith County & Upshur County · Texas

Newton Ballard arrived in Smith County Texas in 1850 and filed a land claim in Upshur County in 1856. The New Hope Missionary Baptist Church history in Smith County named Aaron and Emaline — two members of Newton's community — independently confirming their presence there. If your family was in this community in the 1850s–1870s, especially under the Ballard or Bullard surname, we want to connect.

Copiah County · Mississippi

Lewis F. Ballard Sr. held Newton Ballard and at least seven other enslaved people in Copiah County, Mississippi from approximately 1830 to 1850. The Ballard, Barnes, Byrd, King, and neighboring families of Township 10 Range 8 East are all relevant to this research. We are actively seeking Lewis F. Ballard Sr.'s death record and estate probate in Smith County Texas.

Ouachita Parish · Louisiana

The John Bullard estate in Ouachita Parish Louisiana transferred an enslaved family — Louiza, Jesse, Green, Harriet, Hester, Flora, Texana, Herrod, Abram — to Texas in 1854 and 1856. Nancy S. Bullard, John Bullard's daughter, married into the Sheppard family and inherited this family group. The succession record for John Bullard in Ouachita Parish is an active research target. If you research the Bullard, Faulk, or Sheppard families of Ouachita Parish we want to connect.

Anson County · North Carolina

The Bullard family has been documented in Anson County North Carolina continuously from 1778 through 1893 — with the patriarch John Bullard entering 200 acres on Clark's Creek in 1778. The Bullard/Ballard surname fluidity in Anson County mirrors what Newton Ballard's records show in Texas. We are actively seeking Bullard probate records, will records, and migration deeds for this family through external researcher Norris Douglas.
